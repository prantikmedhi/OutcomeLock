# Demo

## Goal

Show the full OutcomeLock journey in about two minutes.

The judge should understand:

> A grievance response is not the same as the requested outcome being completed.

## Required Disclosure

Display clearly:

> Independent hackathon prototype · Synthetic data · Not a government product

## Demo Path

### 1. Start

Click:

> Try an example case

Expected result: the pension case loads immediately.

### 2. Problem

Show:

> My pension has not been credited for three months.

Explain that OutcomeLock first identifies the citizen's actual desired outcome.

### 3. Confirm Outcome

Show:

> Pension credited to my bank account.

Click:

> Yes, that's what I need

### 4. Government Response

Show:

> The grievance has been forwarded to the concerned office for necessary action.

Status:

> Closed

Click:

> Check if this is actually resolved

### 5. Verdict

Show:

> NOT RESOLVED

Explain:

- You asked for pension credit.
- They confirmed forwarding.
- Evidence of pension credit is missing.

Click:

> Appeal the closure

### 6. Appeal

Show editable appeal text.

Click:

> Submit appeal

Make clear submission is simulated.

### 7. Timeline

Show appeal submitted and review started.

Trigger or continue to synthetic resolution.

### 8. Resolution

Show:

> Pension credit: INR 8,450 on 19 August 2026

Final status:

> RESOLVED

## Demo Requirements

- No login.
- No OTP.
- No real personal information.
- No government API.
- No external setup.
- No dead buttons.
- No unpredictable core AI dependency.
- Works even if OpenAI is unavailable.

## Backup Demo

If OpenAI fails, use the seeded pension scenario and fallback explanation. The deterministic verdict must still work.
