# Product

## Thesis

OutcomeLock measures grievance resolution by citizen outcome, not administrative activity.

Core belief:

> A government complaint should be measured by whether the citizen got the outcome they asked for, not whether someone replied.

## Problem

Citizens often receive grievance responses that sound final but do not prove the issue was fixed. A complaint may be marked closed even when the requested benefit, payment, correction, certificate, or service has not been delivered.

Administrative states such as forwarded, assigned, action taken, disposed, replied, and closed are not the same as outcome completion.

## Product Promise

OutcomeLock helps citizens understand:

- what outcome they actually requested
- what evidence would prove it happened
- whether the response shows that evidence
- what action to take next

## Primary Scenario

A citizen's pension has not been credited for three months.

Requested outcome:

> Pension credited to my bank account.

Synthetic response:

> The grievance has been forwarded to the concerned office for necessary action.

OutcomeLock result:

> Not resolved.

Reason:

> Forwarding the complaint does not prove that the pension was credited.

## MVP Scope

Build:

- citizen problem intake
- AI interpretation of desired outcome
- citizen confirmation
- synthetic grievance response display
- deterministic outcome evaluation
- verdict screen
- explanation
- recommended next action
- appeal generation
- appeal editing
- simulated appeal submission
- timeline
- synthetic resolution
- final resolved state

Do not build:

- real government integrations
- real authentication
- real payments
- Aadhaar, PAN, EPFO, CPGRAMS, or bank integrations
- admin dashboard
- broad government-service marketplace
- notification infrastructure
- native mobile apps

## Positioning

OutcomeLock is not a government portal and not a chatbot wrapper. It is a citizen-side outcome verification layer.

The product should feel independent, calm, practical, and trustworthy.
